# Brand assets

Meta and WhatsApp SVGs: Simple Icons 16.0.0 (CC0 collection).
Source: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.0.0/icons
Files: meta.svg, whatsapp.svg. Brand marks remain trademarks of their respective owners.

360dialog SVG: the company's own Icon pack, from its official brand assets page.
Source: https://360dialog.com/brand/assets (Icon pack -> Icon/Digital/SVG/)
File: 360dialog.svg, the unmodified "360Dialog Icon - White Cutout" variant —
chosen among the shipped colours because the interface is dark: the Perf Green
lockup is a near-black teal (#092f32) and disappeared against the panel.
Stated condition on that page: "Use per brand guidelines for consistency." The
linked Brand Guidelines PDF could not be read as text (font-subset encoded), so
the asset is used in ONE OF THE COLOUR VARIANTS THE COMPANY SHIPS (Black, White,
Perf Green) and is NOT recoloured or redrawn. That is why it renders as an
<img>, not through the currentColor mask the other two use.

Baileys: no logo of its own — the channel is a WhatsApp session, so it carries
the WhatsApp mark, with "Nao oficial" always next to it in the interface.
